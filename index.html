Tests and experiments

Rules in this build (provisional ones marked)

  • FT admits a candidate if its plausibility p ≥ 0.6 · FT_eff · max p. Invalid parses are never admitted. Lowered when the intended reading was filtered out; raised when a higher threshold would have rejected a wrong pick and kept the right one. Provisional.
  • TI sets the memory decay λ = 0.3 + 0.65 · TI_eff, so lower TI = shorter window = faster response (same convention as Receiver Life). Adjusted by comparing short (λ=0.3) and long (λ=0.95) window predictions. Provisional.
  • AR scores admitted candidates by ln p + integrated evidence, and commits only if the top score leads by margin m = 0.35 · (0.5 + Θ of the + slot). Otherwise it reports the ambiguity and asks. AR has no parameter of its own.
  • SG sets the confidence gain (0.5 + 3 · SG_eff) and the weight of evidence written to memory (0.5 + SG_eff). Adjusted for calibration (confidence vs outcome). Confidence never overrides the verifier. Provisional.
  • UE sets the adaptation step η = 0.15 · UE_eff and the switch-attempt probability 0.5 · UE_eff. UE itself changes only by: drive = (ē − 0.25); ΔUE = 0.15 · drive · (1 − UE) if drive > 0, else 0.15 · drive · UE; minus 0.15 · 0.3 · instability · UE. Provisional hypothesis.
  • Slots: each parameter's data channel updates Θ of the slot it occupies. Θ biases the occupant's effective value by 0.3 · (Θ − ½). Q[k][j] tracks reward when parameter j sits in slot k; reassignment chooses among "stay" and the six transpositions by softmax on ΔQ (τ = 0.08).
  • Gaussian mode: (2+i)^2, (3+2i)^2, square of 12+i or square of 7 (read as 7 + i) shows z², its triangle, the mirror vectors, and the primes in the hypotenuse with their two-square and root-pair forms. It is exact and bypasses the receiver.
  • Math in words still uses keyword rules only (no LLM). "Combine A and B" with a [context] tag is the separate add-versus-multiply selection case.
  • Any question (anything the math engine cannot read): Claude lists 1–4 readings with probabilities p and a stable key per sense, without answering. Those readings go through the same FT → TI → AR → SG pipeline as math interpretations: FT drops unlikely readings, TI adds what you chose before for the same ambiguity (keyed like word:bear), AR commits or holds the readings open and asks. Claude then answers only the reading the receiver chose or you picked. General answers are not verified; SG confidence is relevance, not proof. Each question uses your own Claude usage.
  • Learned state (parameters, slots, memory) is kept in this browser between visits. Reset clears it.
